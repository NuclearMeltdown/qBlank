qBlank next to other programs for a capture card, a screen or a window.

Comparisons

qBlank vs OBS Studio

OBS composes scenes for streams. qBlank shows one source to play on and records it. Timing, sound, analogue consoles, screen, recording, and how to use both.

qBlank vs AmaRecTV

Both are small programs to watch and record a capture card. AmaRecTV has stood still since 2017. Display, setup, sound, analogue consoles, screen, recording, keys.

qBlank vs PotPlayer

PotPlayer is a video player that can also open a card. Display, setup, sound, analogue consoles, screen, recording.

qBlank vs NVIDIA App

NVIDIA's app records games on NVIDIA GPUs, with Instant Replay. Capture card, what gets recorded, codecs, replay, keys.

qBlank vs VirtualDub2

A video editor with a capture mode, long used for lossless captures. Display, setup, sound, analogue consoles, screen, recording.

qBlank vs Elgato Studio

Elgato's app for its own cards, after the 4K Capture Utility. Display, setup, sound, analogue consoles, screen, recording.

qBlank vs Streaming Center

AVerMedia's app for streams and recordings with its own devices, after RECentral. Display, setup, sound, analogue consoles, screen, recording, keys.

Side by side

Compared with OBS Studio 33.0.0, AmaRecTV 4.11, PotPlayer 1.7.23164, NVIDIA App 11.0.9, VirtualDub2 2.5.7, Elgato Studio 1.1 and Streaming Center 1.8.22.2. The details are on each comparison page.
